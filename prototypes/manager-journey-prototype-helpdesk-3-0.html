Routing, in four questions.

Today, merchants describe routing with conditional rules — "what to do." In Helpdesk 3.0, the system asks four questions for every ticket. The merchant declares the small surface that actually changes per business.

1What
What is this ticket about?
Intent
A classification of the customer's underlying request — refund, shipping, promo, VIP order.
In Gorgias today
Already exists — but hidden inside AI Agent. H3 makes it the top-level routing key.
2Where
Where does it belong?
Queue
A named bucket of tickets that share routing logic, a team, capacity rules, and SLA targets.
In Gorgias today
Replaces Rules + Views + Teams + Tags — four objects collapse into one.
3When
How urgent vs. others?
Signal
A composite score — customer value, SLA risk, sentiment, channel — that re-ranks tickets inside a queue.
In Gorgias today
Net-new. Replaces manual Priority field + SLA badge with one composite, system-aware score.
4Who
Who can take it right now?
Capacity
Per-agent concurrent limits, tier modifiers, OOO blocking, push-vs-pick behavior.
In Gorgias today
Net-new. Today agents self-select from views; H3 lets the system push respecting limits.
The merchant no longer describes what to do for every condition. They describe what kind of work this is — and the system answers the rest.
